隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — Tonyou

中文

生效日期：2026-10-31

一、概览

「同你游 Tonyou」是一款本地优先的运动与出行轨迹记录工具。本应用不需要注册账号，不收集你的姓名、手机号、通讯录或其他可识别个人身份的信息。应用底部会展示 Google AdMob 广告，广告由 Google 提供，详见第五节。

二、保存在你设备上的信息

  • 轨迹数据：定位坐标、时间、海拔、速度、运动方式、轨迹名称
  • 你主动添加的内容：途中文字笔记、照片（保存在应用私有目录，并另存一份到系统相册 Pictures/Tonyou）
  • 应用设置：语言、字体大小、定位数据来源偏好

以上数据全部保存在本机应用私有存储中，不会自动上传到我们的服务器（本应用没有服务器）。

三、权限与用途

  • 位置信息（仅在使用期间，由带常驻通知的前台服务持续获取）：记录轨迹、绘制实时轨迹线、偏航提醒；应用不申请后台定位权限（ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION）
  • 相机 / 照片：你点「拍照留念」时拍摄并保存照片
  • 通知：显示"正在记录"的前台通知与搜星提示
  • 文件与存储：导出 GPX / KML、导入轨迹文件

权限仅在你使用对应功能时调用，可在系统设置中随时关闭；关闭后相应功能将不可用。

四、共享与导出

只有你主动操作时数据才会离开本机：导出 GPX / KML、点「邮箱备份」把文件发送到你自己的邮箱、或通过系统分享发给你选择的人。这些操作由你发起并由你决定接收方。

五、第三方服务

① 地图底图由 OpenStreetMap 提供。显示地图时，你的设备会向地图瓦片服务器请求图片，服务器可能获知你的 IP 地址与请求的地图范围。② 广告由 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）提供。为了展示与计量广告，SDK 可能收集：广告标识符（Advertising ID，可在系统"设置 → Google → 广告"中重置或删除）、设备型号与系统版本、大致网络与地区信息、广告展示与点击记录。我们不会把这些信息与你的轨迹、笔记或照片关联，也不会把轨迹数据提供给广告商。③ Google 的隐私做法见 https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites 。除此之外，本应用不接入其他统计或社交 SDK。

六、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、数据安全与保留

数据保存在应用私有目录，其他应用无法读取。保留多久完全由你决定：删除轨迹即删除对应数据，卸载应用会删除全部本地数据且无法恢复，请自行备份。

八、你的权利

你可以随时查看、导出、重命名或删除全部数据。由于我们未收集任何数据，无需另行申请数据访问或删除。

九、政策更新

本政策如有变更，我们会更新应用内本页内容并注明生效日期。

十、联系方式

隐私相关问题请联系 qekosync@gmail.com，我们会在 7 个工作日内回复。

English

Effective date: 2026-10-31

1. Overview

Tonyou is a local-first activity and trip recorder. It requires no account and collects no name, phone number, contacts or other personally identifiable information. A Google AdMob banner is shown at the bottom of the app; see section 5.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Track data: coordinates, time, elevation, speed, activity mode, track name
  • Content you add: text notes and photos taken in-app (stored in app-private storage, with a copy saved to the system gallery under Pictures/Tonyou)
  • App settings: language, text size, location-source preference

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone. Nothing is uploaded automatically — the app has no server.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Location (while in use only, continuously read by a foreground service with a persistent notification): record tracks, draw the live path, off-route alerts; the app does not request background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION)
  • Camera / photos: only when you tap "Photo" to capture a waypoint photo
  • Notifications: the ongoing "recording" notification and GPS-fix notice
  • Files: exporting GPX / KML and importing track files

Permissions are used only when you use the related feature and can be revoked in system settings; the related feature will then stop working.

4. Sharing and export

Data leaves your phone only when you start it: exporting GPX / KML, using "Email backup" to send a file to your own address, or sharing via the system sheet. You choose the recipient.

5. Third-party services

① Base maps come from OpenStreetMap. When a map is shown, your device requests tiles from OSM servers, which may see your IP address and the requested map area. ② Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To deliver and measure ads the SDK may collect: your advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Settings → Google → Ads), device model and OS version, coarse network and region information, and ad impression/click events. We never link this to your tracks, notes or photos, and your track data is never passed to advertisers. ③ Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . No other analytics or social SDK is included.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Security and retention

Data lives in app-private storage that other apps cannot read. Retention is entirely up to you: deleting a track deletes its data, and uninstalling the app removes all local data permanently.

8. Your rights

You can view, export, rename or delete all data at any time. Because we collect nothing, no data-access or deletion request is needed.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page in the app and note the effective date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: qekosync@gmail.com — we reply within 7 business days.
